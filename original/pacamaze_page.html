Pacamaze Game Review

Rating: 5 out of 5

Pacamaze is a 3D Pac-man game, where instead of collecting power pellets, you collect ammo and can shoot the ghosts! It's an interesting idea, and although the engine is rather buggy, it does work well enough to play and enjoy. You are given both a mini-map (on screen at all times) showing the ghosts, as well as a full-maze map that you can also see the remaining dots you need to collect. Supposedly there is a end-game boss of some kind, but I didn't make it that far, so you'll need to play yourself to find out! Pretty impressive considering that the zip file is only 33k!
